DESTINATION INSIGHTS

Explore the Destinations Covered in our Kerala Tour Packages

Munnar

Munnar is known for its rolling tea plantations, winding hill roads and cool mountain surroundings. It is a popular choice for couples and families looking for a peaceful break away from busy city life.

Depending on your itinerary, you can explore viewpoints and local attractions such as Mattupetty and Echo Point. Packages with two nights in Munnar allow time to enjoy the hill station before continuing to another destination.

Thekkady

Thekkady offers a nature focused experience, with forest landscapes, spice plantations and access to the Periyar region. It can be a useful addition to a Kerala itinerary that combines hill stations with other experiences.

Several of our longer packages include an overnight stay in Thekkady. Check the individual itinerary for sightseeing activities and any entry fees or optional experiences.

Alleppey and Alappuzha

Alleppey, also known as Alappuzha, is famous for its backwaters, canals and houseboat experiences. A stay here can add a different perspective to a Kerala holiday after exploring Munnar's hills.

Our listed Munnar with Alleppey houseboat tour is designed around a backwater experience. Other packages include an overnight stay in the Alleppey or Alappuzha region. Confirm whether your selected itinerary includes a houseboat or hotel accommodation.

Kochi and Cochin

Kochi, also known as Cochin, combines coastal scenery, heritage attractions and a lively city atmosphere. Visitors often explore Fort Kochi, the Chinese fishing nets and Mattancherry Palace.

Kochi can also serve as an arrival or departure point for a multi destination trip. Packages such as Misty Munnar with Cochin and Wonderful Kerala Fly N Stay include time in this region.

Kovalam

Kovalam is known for its coastal setting and beaches. It can be a relaxing final stop after visiting Kerala's hill stations and backwaters.

Packages such as Exploring Kerala and the Kerala family leisure tour package include Kovalam in their listed itineraries. Check the duration of the stay and the sightseeing arrangements before choosing your package.
