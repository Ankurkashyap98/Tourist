When to Visit What: The Indian Travel Matrix

Timing is everything in the Himalayas and tropical India. Use our verified seasonal calendar to lock in the perfect month for your dream trip.

Peak: Mar – Jun & Dec – Feb

Kashmir Valley

Spring Tulips (Apr) • Winter Snow (Dec-Feb)

Dal Lake Shikara rides, Gulmarg Phase 2 snow Gondola cable car, Pahalgam pine valleys, and authentic Wazwan dinners in houseboats.

Peak: May – September

Ladakh High Passes

Summer Mountain Passes (Khardung La)

Turquoise Pangong Tso camps, Nubra Valley double-humped camel safaris, ancient monasteries, and clear starry Himalayan skies.

Peak: Mar – Jun & Dec – Feb

Himachal (Shimla/Manali)

Summer Escape • Atal Tunnel Snow

Solang Valley adventure sports, Atal Tunnel crossing into snowy Sissu, Hadimba temple cedar trails, and scenic British Shimla Mall Road.

Peak: September – March

Kerala Backwaters

Pleasant Winter • Post-Monsoon Greenery

Misty Munnar tea plantations, private thatched houseboat cruises in Alleppey, Ayurvedic wellness retreats, and Periyar elephant safaris.

Peak: October – March

Royal Rajasthan

Pleasant Winter • Desert Festivals

Amber Fort elephant rides in Jaipur, Mehrangarh Fort views in Jodhpur, Lake Pichola sunset boat cruises in Udaipur, and desert dune camping.

Peak: Oct – April (Dubai) / Year-Round (Bali)

Dubai & Bali Luxury

Shopping Festivals • Tropical Beach Villas

Burj Khalifa 124th floor tickets, 4x4 dune bashing desert safaris, Bali private pool villas with floating breakfast, and Nusa Penida speedboats.
