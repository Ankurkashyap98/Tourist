SOLO TRAVEL FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Solo Trip Packages

The best destination depends on your budget, interests and travel experience. Manali and Rishikesh are popular for mountain scenery and outdoor activities, Goa for beaches, Jaipur for culture and heritage, and Kerala for nature and relaxed holidays. Choose a destination that fits your travel dates, budget and preferred activities.

A solo trip in India can cost from a few thousand rupees for a short local getaway to ₹15,000–₹30,000 or more for a multi-day holiday. Your total budget depends on the destination, transport, accommodation, meals, activities and trip duration. Booking transport early and choosing practical accommodation can help control costs.

For a budget of ₹5,000, consider short trips to nearby destinations such as Rishikesh, Haridwar, Jaipur or Agra, depending on transport costs and travel dates. This budget may cover a basic short trip, but accommodation, activities and return transport can increase the total. Check the complete package inclusions before booking.

Choose a nearby destination, travel during the off-season when practical, book transport early and compare hostels or budget accommodation. Use public transport where suitable, plan free or low-cost activities, and set a daily spending limit. Always keep some money aside for emergencies.

For first-time solo travellers, Rishikesh, Jaipur, Udaipur, Goa, Kerala and parts of Himachal Pradesh are worth considering. Each offers a different experience, from culture and beaches to nature and adventure. Check seasonal conditions, transport access and local safety information before choosing your destination.

Travelling by train or bus, choosing budget accommodation, visiting nearby destinations and avoiding peak-season dates can help reduce costs. Compare the total journey cost rather than just the ticket price, and check whether a package offers better value than booking each service separately.

Women travelling alone may consider destinations such as Rishikesh, Jaipur, Udaipur, parts of Kerala and well-connected areas of Himachal Pradesh, depending on their experience and comfort level. Research the specific location, choose accommodation with reliable recent reviews, plan arrivals during daylight where possible, and share your itinerary with someone you trust. Safety varies by area and circumstances, so check current local guidance before travelling.
