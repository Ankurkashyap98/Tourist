The Trip Departure Story

Why We Started In Jammu &
How We Redefined Travel

Traveling through Kashmir, Ladakh, or Himachal isn't just about booking a hotel room online. Mountain weather is unpredictable, mountain roads demand veteran drivers, and true Kashmiri or Ladakhi warmth comes only through trusted local connections.

Recognizing that travelers were tired of faceless booking portals and misleading photos, Trip Departure was founded in Shiv Nagar, Jammu. We inspect every hotel room ourselves, hand-pick traditional Dal Lake pine houseboats, and assign polite, vetted drivers who act as protective guides for your family.

"Our mission is simple: treat every guest who comes to Kashmir, Ladakh, or anywhere in India as if they were visiting our own personal home."

— Ashish M., Founder of Trip Departure
